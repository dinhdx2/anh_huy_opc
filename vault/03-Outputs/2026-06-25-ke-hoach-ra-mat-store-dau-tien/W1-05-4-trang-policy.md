## Our Commitment

At **Daily Humor Co.**, every product is **made to order** specifically for you. Because each item is printed and produced on demand, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for reasons such as change of mind, wrong size ordered, or dislike of the design.

We stand behind the quality of our products. If something goes wrong on our end — we will make it right.

## What We Cover (Eligible for Refund or Reprint)

You are eligible for a **free reprint or full refund** if:

- The item arrives **damaged** (torn, broken, or unusable).
- The item has a **printing defect** (blurry print, misaligned design, color bleed, blank area).
- You received the **wrong item** (wrong design, wrong product type, or wrong color/size compared to your order).
- The item is **lost in transit** and never arrives (see shipping policy for timeframes).

To be eligible, you must contact us at **hello@dailyhumorco.com** within **30 days** of the delivery date (or estimated delivery date if lost) with:

1. Your order number.
2. A clear photo or video showing the issue.
3. A brief description of the problem.

We reserve the right to request additional information to verify the claim.

## What We Do Not Cover (Not Eligible)

- **Buyer's remorse** — you changed your mind after ordering.
- **Wrong size or color selected** — please review our size guide carefully before ordering. Sizes vary by product and brand; we are not responsible for incorrect selections.
- **Minor color variations** — slight differences between screen display and printed color are normal and are not considered defects.
- **Customization errors entered by the buyer** (if applicable) — if you provided custom text or an uploaded file, we print exactly what was submitted.
- **Damage caused after delivery** — wash damage, accidental damage, or normal wear and tear.

## Refund & Reprint Process

Once your claim is approved (typically within **2–3 business days** of receiving your request), we will:

- Issue a **full refund** to your original payment method, OR
- Send a **free reprint** to the same shipping address on file.

We will confirm which remedy applies via email. Refunds may take **5–10 business days** to appear on your statement depending on your bank or payment provider.

## Return Shipments

Because our products are made to order, **we do not require you to return the item** in most defect/damage cases. We may request return of the item at our expense in specific circumstances; we will provide a prepaid label if so.

## Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled **within 24 hours** of placement at no charge. After 24 hours, production may have begun and cancellation is no longer guaranteed. Contact us immediately at **hello@dailyhumorco.com** if you need to cancel.

## Contact

Questions about your order? Email us at **hello@dailyhumorco.com**. We aim to respond within **1–2 business days**.
